Sorting incoming mail

Work through each check in order. The first category that matches is where the mail goes.

🗑 Toss junk mail before sorting
If it's obvious junk, throw it away — don't waste time sorting it. Toss anything that looks like:

· "Current Resident" or "Current Business Owner" with no specific name
· Generic bulk mailers, mass ads, or promotional catalogs
· Unsolicited credit card or insurance offers
· Political mailers with no specific addressee

If you're unsure whether something is junk, go ahead and sort it — when in doubt, don't toss it.

Member mail pickups

How to handle it when a member walks in to collect their mail.

Anytime & Virtual Mailbox — walk-ins
1
Ask for their name
If the name doesn't match anything, ask for their company name instead.
2
Look them up in the VM_Anytime tab
Google Sheet Find their box number.
3
Pull their mail and hand it over
Go to the file cabinet, pull everything from their slot, hand it to the member.
Physical mailbox members
They have their own keys to their physical boxes — they let themselves in. No action needed unless they have a scan/forward flag, which you handle at sorting time.
iPostal members
Pickups and scan requests come through the iPostal app to-do list. Check daily. Open the item, scan it in the app, put it back in their file.

Friday mail forwarding

Every Friday — collect, ship, then bill. Do all three before end of day.

1
Collect forwarding mail from the bottom drawer
Right side of the desk, bottom cabinet — all envelopes from the week.
2
Create labels in USPS Click-N-Ship
For each forwarding member with mail:
1. Click New Label
2. Select USPS Flat Rate Envelope
3. Weigh the envelope
4. Enter the forwarding address from the sheet
5. Create the label — repeat for each member
3
Pay for all labels at once
Cart → Select All → Pay Now → check the box → Credit Card
CVV: 469 → Print and Pay → Print Labels
4
Tape labels to envelopes and drop at USPS
5
Bill each member in Kube (Yardi)
Repeat for every member you shipped today.

Tour questions

Use these to understand what a prospect needs before walking them through the space. Keep it a conversation — not an interrogation.

Opening — why they're here
  • What prompted you to come in / call today? What does the ideal outcome look like for you?
  • Tell me about your business — what do you do, how long have you been at it, what got you started?
Where they work now
  • Where does your business operate from currently?
  • Who are your customers? Do you meet with them in person — where, how often, how many people?
  • Do you have any other regular meetings?
What they're looking for
  • Are you looking for a dedicated full-time space, or something flexible / as-needed?
  • Do you prefer an open coworking environment or a private office?
Why they're making a change
  • Why are you thinking about making a change?
  • If working from home: What are your biggest challenges with that setup?
  • If coming from a competitor: What do they do well? What could they do better?
  • When do you anticipate making the move?
Decision & budget
  • Is this just for you, or do you have a team to consider?
  • Besides yourself, is anyone else involved in the decision?
  • What kind of budget do you have in mind?
Before the walkthrough
  • Is there anything else I haven't asked that you'd like to share before I show you around?
During the tour: parking → directory → lobby → coffee → available spaces → amenities. Then transition to contract creation if they're ready.

Daily schedule

Your day from open to close. Follow this order every day.

Morning — opening up
  • 1
    Turn on all lights — front desk hanging lights, both conference rooms, mail room, shared space hanging lights (×2), kitchen.
  • 2
    Turn on the TV — press OK, tune to any channel.
  • 3
    Check the dishwasher — if clean, put dishes away. If dirty, run it.
  • 4
    Check the coffee rack — fill if not full.
  • 5
    Wipe the counter if dirty.
  • 6
    Check front desk for weekend drop-off mail — hold it until midday delivery arrives, then distribute everything together.
  • 7
    Open the file cabinet. Set up the front TV slideshow: Source → Monitor → Click first picture → Play. Confirm slideshow is running.
  • 8
    Open Yardi Kube → Bookings tab. If there's a reservation today, make sure the conference room is tidy and ready.
  • 9
    Check Outlook — respond to emails.
  • 10
    Check HubSpot — follow up with recent contacts, review scheduled tours.
  • 11
    Check the phone for voicemails — listen and respond.
During the day
  • 12
    Going to lunch? Put up the "Stepped Away" sign.
  • 13
    Check iPostal app to-do list — handle any pickup or scan requests.
  • 14
    If you have questions — check Base Camp.
  • 15
    When midday mail arrives — distribute using the mail sorting guide. Include any weekend drop-off held from the morning.
Phone call script
Answer: "Office Evolution, this is ______, how may I help you?"

Potential lead? Get first + last name, company (if possible), email, phone number.

Asking for the owner? "He's unavailable right now — can I take a message?" Most of the time they hang up.
End of day — closing
  • 16a
    Check Yardi Kube bookings for tomorrow — know what to expect before you leave.
  • 16b
    Close both conference rooms.
  • 16c
    Turn off hanging lights, run the dishwasher, turn off the TV.
